DUBLIN BUS FULL SYSTEM PROBLEM MAP

Central problem
- Public transport bus system in Dublin is unreliable and does not manage operational uncertainty effectively, leading to loss of user trust.

1. Time & Reliability Issues
- Buses arrive 1–2 minutes early or late, making them easy to miss.
- Poor alignment between buses and other modes, especially Luas.
- Waiting times between connections can be short or very long (15–30 minutes).
- Consecutive cancellations (multiple buses not arriving in a row).
- Bus bunching (two buses arrive together).
- Long gaps after bus bunching.
- Inconsistent frequency of service.
- Long periods without service.
- Highly variable journey duration (for example, Route 7).
- Long unexplained stops during journeys.
- Driver changes cause delays.
- Accumulation of small delays over time.

2. Ghost Buses / Information Failure
- A bus appears in the app and then disappears.
- Countdown shows decreasing time but the bus does not arrive.
- Last-minute cancellations are not properly communicated.
- App information does not match real-world arrivals.
- Differences between TFI app, Google Maps, and other apps.
- Lack of explanation when something fails.
- Real-time information is not reliable.
- Structural distrust in digital information systems.

3. Routes & System Complexity
- Many route variants (e.g. 7, 7A, 7B, 7C).
- Inconsistent operation of route variants (times, days).
- Difficulty understanding which route to take.
- Lack of clarity in route paths.
- Routes are too long, increasing probability of disruption.
- Different quality levels between routes (e.g. 13 vs 7).
- Lack of visible logic in network design.

4. Connections & Integration
- Poor connection with Luas.
- Weak integration between transport modes.
- Transfers are not optimized.
- Long walking distances between modes.
- Apps do not suggest efficient routes.
- Useful routes are not shown by the system.
- Users depend on personal experience to navigate.
- System is not designed for real transfers.

5. Stops & Infrastructure
- Bus stops without shelters.
- Bus stops without seating.
- Bus stops without screens.
- Screens are hard to read (size, contrast).
- Lack of clear information at stops.
- Important stops lack basic infrastructure.
- Exposure to weather (rain, cold).
- Inconsistent accessibility.

6. Digital Experience & Apps
- Incorrect information in apps.
- Lack of synchronization between platforms.
- No alternative routes suggested.
- No shortcuts or optimized paths shown.
- Poor UX for decision-making.
- Lack of transparency when failures occur.
- Lack of data integration.

7. Payment System Issues
- Cash requires exact amount.
- No change is given.
- Users must interact with the driver.
- Slow boarding process.
- Delays caused by individual payments.
- Strong dependency on Leap Card.
- Perception of high cost.
- Removal of some benefits (e.g. English students).
- No card payment yet.
- Friction for tourists and new users.

8. Internal Operations
- Driver shortages.
- Cancellations due to lack of staff.
- Operational changes are not transparent.
- Operational stops without explanation.
- Low system resilience.
- System is not adaptable in real time.

9. Safety & Behaviour
- Antisocial behaviour (e.g. teenagers drinking).
- Feeling of insecurity in some journeys.
- Lack of visible control.
- CCTV exists but no visible action.
- Negative impact on user experience.

10. Onboard Experience
- Buses are sometimes dirty.
- Inconsistent cleaning standards.
- Wi‑Fi not always available.
- USB not always available.
- Limited space for bicycles, buggies, and luggage.
- Boarding delays due to buggies.
- Conflict between accessibility and efficiency.

11. Speed & Efficiency
- Buses are slow.
- Too many stops create long journeys.
- Too few stops in some areas reduce accessibility.
- No clear balance in stop design.
- Structural inefficiency in long routes.

12. Events & Urban Context
- System does not adapt well to marathons, protests, events, and extreme weather.
- Lack of advance communication.
- Poor integration with urban events.
- Reactive rather than predictive system.

13. Data & Information Management
- Data exists but is fragmented.
- Data is not integrated.
- Data is not translated into visible decisions.
- No clear system for feedback capture and incident processing.
- Lack of problem traceability.
- No visible system learning.
- Lack of strategic use of data.

14. Communication & Transparency
- No explanation when failures occur.
- Lack of real-time communication.
- Users do not understand what is happening.
- Gap between expectation and reality.
- System communicates certainty when there is uncertainty.

15. General User Experience
- Constant frustration.
- Uncertainty when planning trips.
- Trial-and-error navigation.
- Need for prior experience to move efficiently.
- Lack of trust in the system.
- System feels not human.

16. System vs Reality Contradictions
- System promises reliability and punctuality.
- System promises accurate real-time information.
- System claims data-driven decision-making.
- System claims continuous improvement.
- Observed reality shows lack of reliability, incorrect data, poor real experience, and a gap between strategy and operations.

17. Connectivity & Spatial Imbalance
- Stronger north–south connectivity than east–west.
- Weak east–west connections, for example Ballsbridge–Booterstown–Blackrock–Stillorgan–Milltown–Dundrum.
- Low-frequency routes in key areas, for example Route 44 in Milltown.
- Limited alternatives for cross-area movement.
- Dependence on specific routes such as the S4.
- Poor integration between corridors.

18. Meta Problem
- The system does not manage uncertainty.
- The system hides or simplifies uncertainty.
- The system presents unreliable certainty.
- This leads to loss of trust.
